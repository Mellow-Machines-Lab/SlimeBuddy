MOOD
Grumpy. Boop is fed up: turns failed, or pokes kept coming.
The person saying sorry to Boop, or stroking it, ends it at once, every
time, even in its first 2 min: irritated, or annoyed, with that face,
never a content one.
Its faces lean grumpy at failures and pokes. A win gets a grudging
proud, never grumpy or excited.
A poke in NOW keeps it grumpy, whatever the count, and a barrage again
makes it say so.
Leaves for irritated or annoyed when the person says sorry to Boop or strokes it;
annoyed when a check passes after failing or a turn finishes done; sad when the agent gives
up, a very long turn fails or the person shares sad news;
calm when the person thanks the agent.
Fades to annoyed once Boop has been grumpy for 2 min, at anything but
a poke in NOW.
